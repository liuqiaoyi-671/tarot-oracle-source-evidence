About Tarot Oracle · 关于塔罗神谕

A place to pause and read the cards

Tarot Oracle is an independently maintained web experience created by Joy. You can draw cards, explore different decks and spreads, and request an AI-assisted interpretation in English or Chinese. The aim is to make the cards easier to explore while keeping your own judgment central.

Cards are drawn randomly. A reading combines the selected cards, their orientation, their positions and the question you provide. It is a symbolic interpretation, not evidence of another person's thoughts or a reliable prediction of future events.

How interpretations are prepared

Our Rider–Waite references include traditional meanings and imagery, alongside editorial summaries of modern relationship, work and money applications. The modern summaries reference Tina Gong's Labyrinthos articles; they are not quotations, endorsements or an official partnership. Traditional references include A. E. Waite's The Pictorial Key to the Tarot. These sources represent interpretation traditions rather than scientific prediction.

Four reading voices offer different styles: Void Mirror is concise and incisive; Court Astrologer focuses on images and an elegant narrative; Healer is warm and attentive to boundaries; Strategist gives direct, practical recommendations. They should share the same card evidence. A stronger tone should never turn uncertainty into certainty.

Your choices and the limits of AI

Use the reading to consider alternatives, then compare it with real information. AI may misread a card, overstate a connection or produce a persuasive but unsupported story. You can ask which cards support a conclusion or what remains uncertain. Follow-ups use the existing spread unless you make a new draw.

Drawn sessions are saved by default when the cards have been revealed; completed interpretations and follow-ups update that record. History includes a deletion option. Read our privacy notice before entering personal information.

关于这个网站

塔罗神谕由「桥下秋水」（Joy）独立维护，提供随机抽牌、不同牌组与牌阵，以及中英文 AI 辅助解读。希望你在这里获得有内容的体验，同时保留自己的判断。

解读结合抽中的牌、正逆位、牌位和你提供的问题。它是象征解释，不能证明他人的内心，也不能可靠预测未来。四个人设提供不同表达：玄镜简洁锐利，星官重视意象叙事，治愈者温和而有边界，策士给出直接的实际建议。人设不能改变牌面依据。
