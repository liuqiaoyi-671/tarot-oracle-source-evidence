Follow up and reflect · 怎样追问和复盘

Ask for the part that is still unclear

A follow-up should move the conversation forward. If the first reading suggests a boundary, ask what that boundary could look like in your situation. If it gives a broad answer, ask which card and position most strongly support it. Repeating the original question in different words often adds little information.

In Tarot Oracle, follow-ups use the existing spread and include the initial interpretation. They do not secretly draw new cards. A new question may exceed the original spread's scope; in that case, a useful answer says what is missing rather than inventing another card or retelling the whole reading.

A worked example: from advice to a next step

Suppose a reading says, “Avoid taking on all the responsibility for this project.” A useful follow-up is: “I am the only person available this week. Which tasks should I clarify with the team before accepting them?” That adds a practical constraint. The response can distinguish essential tasks, negotiable work and what the cards cannot decide without knowing the actual workload.

Another useful follow-up is, “What would change your interpretation?” The answer might identify a fact worth checking, such as whether a colleague has agreed to take over a task. That makes the uncertainty testable in real life. Asking, “But surely it will all work out?” provides no comparable evidence.

Allow corrections

An AI interpretation can be wrong. If it describes an image that does not appear on your deck, point that out. If it confuses a position, ask it to correct the reading. Consistency with the first answer is less important than fixing a real error. A revised answer should say why it changed rather than quietly replacing one story with another.

Facts can change the application of a card. A recommendation to wait may be less useful once a real deadline is introduced. A wish for a different outcome should not have the same effect. This distinction helps keep the conversation from simply agreeing with each new message.

Use history as a record, not a verdict

When all cards in a session have been revealed, Tarot Oracle saves a history record by default. Successful AI interpretations and follow-ups update the same record. Guest history stays in the browser; signed-in history may be stored with the account. A failed AI request is not a finished interpretation.

When reviewing a reading, compare the original question, what you knew then and what you did next. Notice which observations were useful and which were vague or unsupported. A later event that resembles one phrase does not establish that the entire reading predicted it. It is easy to remember matches and overlook misses.

Know when to pause

Repeatedly redrawing until a preferred card appears changes the exercise. You are selecting reassurance rather than observing a random draw. If you feel stuck, write down the unresolved real-world question and leave the reading for a while. Direct communication, practical information or appropriate professional help may address it better.

You can delete saved sessions from history. For account-data questions, use the contact page. A reading is something you can revisit or discard; it does not create an obligation to follow its advice.

追问应当推进问题

首次解读提到边界，可以问边界具体怎样表达；判断太笼统，可以问哪张牌、哪个牌位支持它。追问沿用原牌阵，不会偷偷重新抽牌；新问题超出原牌阵范围时，应说明不足，而不是重复整套牌义。

例子：把建议变成下一步

解读说「不要独自承担项目全部责任」，你可以补充「这周只有我有空，接下任务前需要向团队确认什么」。这增加了真实约束，可帮助区分必要任务、可协商工作及信息不足。也可以问「什么事实会改变这个解读」，找出现实里值得核实的点。

如果 AI 描述了当前牌组没有的意象，或误读牌位，应明确纠正。保持前后说法一致不应优先于修正错误。新的截止日期等事实可以改变建议，仅表达想听另一个结果不构成证据。

历史用于复盘

翻完本轮牌后默认保存，成功解读和追问更新同一记录；游客历史在当前浏览器，登录历史可保存在账号下。复盘时比较当时的问题、已知信息和之后的行动，同时看有用观察与没有依据的句子，不只记住碰巧吻合的部分。

反复抽到满意结果才停，相当于筛选安慰。困住时可以暂停，转向实际沟通与信息收集。历史可以删除，解读不会形成必须采纳建议的义务。
