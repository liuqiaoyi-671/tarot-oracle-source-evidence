Privacy notice · 隐私说明

Updated: 26 October 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

Accounts and service operation

Tarot Oracle uses your email address for account access, verification and account-related messages. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable passwords. Account records include reading credits, membership expiry, verification status and usage records. Login uses a session cookie. Requests may use IP-derived identifiers for abuse prevention and usage limits.

Reading content and AI services

Your question, drawn cards and relevant conversation are sent for processing when you request an AI interpretation. The official channel sends these through our service to DeepSeek. When you select Zhipu, our service forwards your API key, question and conversation to the fixed official Zhipu endpoint to support browser access; this forwarding handler does not save the key or log request content. Other custom AI providers or relays receive requests from your browser at the selected endpoint. Its own privacy terms also apply. Avoid including information that you do not want those providers to process.

A custom API key is stored in this browser's local storage and used in requests to your selected endpoint. Exported settings backups can also contain this key; do not share a backup with others. It is therefore not accurate to say that a key is never transmitted: the provider or relay needs it to authenticate your request. Only enter keys for services and endpoints you trust.

History and browser storage

Revealed sessions are saved by default. History can contain your question, cards, interpretation and follow-up conversation. Guest history and preferences use browser storage; signed-in history may be stored with your account. You can remove individual sessions or clear history using the history controls. Browser data also holds preferences and anonymous or session identifiers used for usage limits and site analytics.

Site analytics and infrastructure

The site records usage events such as opening the app, selecting a spread and requesting a reading, along with language, device category, referring domain and anonymous/session identifiers. Signed-in events can be associated with the account ID. These records help us understand use of the service. We use Cloudflare for hosting and service infrastructure, email providers for account messages, and Google Fonts for fonts on the main app. External services can receive request metadata when your browser connects to them.

Payments

When PayPal checkout is enabled, payment details are handled on PayPal’s website under its own privacy terms. We store the website account ID, order and payment references, amount, currency, status and credit adjustments for fulfillment, refunds and reconciliation. We do not receive or store your full bank-card number. Your tarot questions and readings are not included in payment requests.

Advertising

No Google advertising script is enabled as of this update. If Google ads are enabled later, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow it and its partners to deliver personalized ads. You can manage personalization through Google My Ad Center and read Google's advertising information. Where required, advertising will use an appropriate consent mechanism before being enabled.

Questions and deletion requests

Use our contact page to ask about stored account data or request deletion. History controls remove visible saved sessions; related service records, usage limits, transaction records or infrastructure backups may have separate retention needs. We do not promise that deleting a visible session instantly removes every operational record or backup.

账号、解读与历史

邮箱用于登录、验证和账号相关通知；密码保存为哈希值。账号记录包含次数、会员到期时间、验证状态和使用记录。登录使用会话 Cookie，服务可能使用与 IP 相关的标识限制滥用与使用次数。

请求 AI 解读时，问题、抽牌信息和相关对话会发送给处理服务。官方通道通过本站服务调用 DeepSeek；选择智谱时，本站服务将密钥、问题和对话转发给智谱官方固定接口，以支持浏览器访问；转发程序不保存密钥、不记录请求正文。其他自选 AI 或中转由浏览器发往所选接口，该服务自身的隐私条款也适用。请勿输入不希望它们处理的私人信息。

自选 API 密钥保存于本机浏览器 localStorage，并会用于向所选服务或中转发起的认证请求；导出的设置备份也可能包含密钥，请勿将备份分享给他人。因此不能说密钥永远不会传输，只应向你信任的接口提供密钥。

翻牌完成后默认保存历史，可能含问题、牌面、解读和追问。游客记录在浏览器，登录记录可保存至账号。可以删除单条或清空历史。浏览器也保存偏好、使用限额和统计相关的匿名／会话标识。

支付

PayPal 付款入口开放后，支付信息由 PayPal 页面处理，其自身隐私条款适用。本站保存网站账号 ID、订单及支付编号、金额、币种、状态和次数变动，用于到账发放、退款和核对；不接收或保存完整银行卡号，也不会把塔罗问题或解读发送到付款接口。

统计、基础服务与广告

本站记录打开页面、选择牌阵、请求解读等使用事件，以及语言、设备类别、来源域名和匿名／会话标识。登录事件可关联账号 ID。Cloudflare 提供托管与服务基础设施，邮件服务用于账号通知，主应用通过 Google Fonts 获取字体。浏览器连接外部服务时，它们可能收到请求相关信息。

截至本次更新，尚未启用 Google 广告脚本。若后续启用，Google 及其他广告供应商可能根据你对本站或其他网站的访问使用 Cookie 提供广告。你可通过上方 Google 链接管理个性化设置；需要时会在启用前接入适当的同意机制。

账号数据与删除请求请使用联系页面。历史删除入口移除可见保存记录；交易、限额、安全记录和基础设施备份可能有不同保留需求，不承诺删除记录瞬间清除所有相关服务数据。
